## 5. URL Routing and Slug Policy

- English pages use clean, descriptive kebab-case:
  `/en/biography`, `/en/education/dasavadani`, `/en/books/shambala`, `/en/institutions`, `/en/recognition`, `/en/inner-journey`.
- Tamil pages mirror the identical routing hierarchy with `/ta/` prefix:
  `/ta/biography`, `/ta/education/dasavadani`, `/ta/books/shambala`, `/ta/institutions`, `/ta/recognition`, `/ta/inner-journey`.
- This ensures clean canonical URL mapping, flawless `hreflang` cross-linking, and universal accessibility across browsers and AI crawlers.
