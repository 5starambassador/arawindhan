## 2. Official Channel Architecture & Standards

### Channel 1: YouTube (@DrJArawindhanOfficial)
- **Primary Content:** Keynote addresses to school principals (e.g., "ACHARIYA's WOW Factors"), whole-brain pedagogy explanations, Maha Shambala discourses, and student world-record demonstrations.
- **Standards:** Verified custom thumbnails, professional English and Tamil closed captions (CC), detailed video descriptions with timestamps, and canonical link pointing to `arawindhan.org`.

### Channel 2: LinkedIn (@Dr-J-Arawindhan)
- **Primary Content:** Educational leadership, enterprise architecture, "One Parent, Four Children" model, Centre of Excellence governance, and Future Ready 2035 strategies.
- **Audience:** School principals, university chancellors, corporate leaders, and education policymakers.

### Channel 3: Instagram & Facebook (@DrJArawindhanOfficial)
- **Primary Content:** Daily WOW micro-practices, Golden Smile Meditation prompts, Avadhani lifestyle tips, student achievement spotlights, and quotes from his 7 published works.
- **Tone:** Inspiring, grounded, family-oriented, and bilingual.

## 4. Governance & Quality Guardrails

- **Zero Bot / Fake Engagement:** No automated follow/unfollow schemes, purchased comments, or engagement pods.
- **Factual Integrity Review:** No clip or quote card may be published unless verified against `MASTER_CLAIMS_REGISTER.csv`.
- **Transcript Verification:** All video transcripts must be human-reviewed for accurate pedagogical terminology (e.g., Dasavadani, Heguru, ConCardios, Seyalthiram).
