## 2. Verification Status Definitions

All claims, figures, affiliations, and historical milestones stored in the platform's knowledge registers must carry one of the following designations:

1. **`CONFIRMED`**:
   - Supported by primary documentary evidence (e.g., registered institution deeds, publisher records, official gazettes, university accreditations like NAAC/ISO certificates, verified public awards with verifiable citations).
2. **`SOURCE_CLAIM`**:
   - Explicitly documented in internal institutional records, portfolio presentations, or authorized profile dossiers, but pending external third-party registry cross-checking.
   - *Public Presentation Rule:* Must be framed with careful attribution (e.g., *"According to the institutional portfolio...", "As documented in ACHARIYA system records..."*).
3. **`PENDING_VERIFICATION`**:
   - Specific dates, ISBNs, exact roll counts, or partner names where additional archival or documentary evidence is required prior to public presentation.
4. **`CONFLICTING`**:
   - Figures or statements where different internal documents or timelines offer differing counts (e.g., 61 companies vs 67+ companies, 240 vs 302+ initiatives).
   - *Public Presentation Rule:* Preserves both source figures transparently and provides context on the differing scopes/dates rather than choosing the larger number.
5. **`APPROVAL_REQUIRED`**:
   - Personal biographical details, spiritual reflections, family lineage, or forward-looking commitments that require direct sign-off from Dr. J. Arawindhan Ji or designated representatives.
6. **`RESTRICTED`**:
   - Confidential internal operating SOPs, private contact details, proprietary teacher-training rubrics, or unpublished manuscripts. **Never to be published to public web pages.**

## 3. Handling Specific Categories

- **Schools & Colleges:** 18 colleges and 101 schools across 4 growth models (Owned, Powered, Partnered, Parented). Both aggregate counts and model breakdowns must be provided so readers understand the relationship structure.

- **Student & Parent Reach:** Student counts (14,000+), parents empowered (10,000+), educators & staff (2,000+) are categorized as `SOURCE_CLAIM` verified against current institutional MIS records.

### 3.2 Awards & World Records
- **Guinness & World Records:** Must be cited precisely as "600+ world records achieved by students across open and blindfolded memory/skill challenges, including 222 documented Guinness and World Records".
- **National & State Honours:** Citations must list the conferring body (e.g., *News7 Tamil Aatral Migu Asiriyar Virudhugal 2026*, *Times Interact*, *The Times of India*, *Global Economic Council*, *JCI India*, *Infinite Thoughts*).
- **Prohibited:** Never invent dates, ranks, or prestigious titles without an explicit issuing citation.

### 3.3 Books & Publications
- Detail individual titles: *The Shambala Book*, *Iravadh Book*, *WOW Parenting*, *Financial Intelligence (FI)*, *Emotional Intelligence (EI)*, *Master Stroke*, and *Young Scientist*.
- Include ISBN and publisher details only where primary copyright documentation is verified. Missing fields must be left blank or marked "In ecosystem circulation / Forthcoming edition".

### 3.4 Separation of Domains
To maintain academic and professional authority while honoring his spiritual path:
- **Educational Innovations** (Dasavadani, MiWay, Q-Math, LABS, TRACK) must be presented with pedagogical structure, target age groups, learning objectives, and operational mechanics.
- **Spiritual & Inner Development** (Santoshi Shri Arawindhan Ji, Divya Yogam, Divine Grace Foundation, Shambala) must be maintained in its own dedicated section with philosophical dignity, without conflating academic credentials with spiritual teachings.

## 4. Corrections and Updates Protocol

1. **Submission:** Inconsistencies or new verification certificates can be submitted directly through the editorial coordinator.
2. **Review Window:** Claims marked `PENDING_VERIFICATION` are audited bi-monthly.
3. **Public Corrections:** If a public statement is updated or revised, the change is recorded in the platform changelog with date and rationale.
